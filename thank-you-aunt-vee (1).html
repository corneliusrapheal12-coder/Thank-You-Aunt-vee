Thank You, Aunt Vee

FROM AYOOLUWA, WITH ALL MY HEART

Aunt Vee,

I have tried to write this so many times in my head, and I always get stuck, because there is no way to fit everything into a few lines. So I will just say it the way I would if you were sitting right in front of me.

Thank you.

Thank you for believing in me before I had done anything to deserve it. Thank you for being the person I can run to when things get tight, and for never making me feel like a burden when I did. Whatever I am doing, whatever I am trying to become, I know you are somewhere behind it, quietly making it possible.

I cannot say thank you without naming the real things you have done, because I never want you to think I do not notice. You give me my allowance. You pay my school fees. You pay my rent, so I always have a roof over my head. You paid for my full stack engineering course, which is opening a whole future for me. You got me a very good laptop so I can actually do the work. And there are so many other things I will not even try to list, because the list would never end. You do it all so quietly that you probably do not think about it anymore. I think about it all the time.

People see Diamond Footwears and they see a brand. I see a woman who works hard, plans ahead, and still finds a way to carry me along with everything else on her plate. You never made a speech about it. I just watched how you carry yourself, how you handle pressure, how you keep going on the days I know are heavy, and I learned from it. That has been my biggest lesson of all.

I do not say it enough, so let me say it properly here. You are my sister, my sponsor, my motivation and my example. When I feel like giving up, I think of you and I remember what is possible. I promise I will not waste what you have put into me. One day I will make you proud in a big way, and when that day comes, I will be right there to carry you the way you have carried me.

May God bless you, protect you and keep your hands full of every good thing. May Diamond Footwears go further than you ever imagined, and may everything you have given out come back to you ten times over.

Ẹ ṣé gan-an, Aunt Vee. I love you dearly.

Always yours,
Ayooluwa
